# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As members and contributors of the **Data Quality & Pipeline Observability Platform (ObsiFlow)** project, we are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, diverse, and respectful collaborative environment. We pledge that all participants will be treated with dignity and respect, regardless of experience level, background, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, or religion.

---

## Standards of Behavior

### Positive Behaviors
We encourage behaviors that contribute to a positive, productive team environment:
- **Respectful Communication**: Treat team members and external contributors with empathy, courtesy, and professional kindness.
- **Constructive Feedback**: Offer thoughtful, actionable suggestions during code reviews, architectural debates, and issue discussions.
- **Collaborative Problem Solving**: Focus on finding collective solutions rather than assigning individual blame.
- **Accountability**: Gracefully accept constructive criticism and take responsibility for mistakes.
- **Shared Growth**: Support and mentor teammates, acknowledging that each role brings specialized domain expertise.

### Unacceptable Behaviors
The following behaviors are strictly unacceptable:
- Demeaning, insulting, or derogatory comments in discussions, pull request reviews, or issue trackers.
- Personal attacks, trolling, harassment, or public humiliation.
- Publishing private information of others without explicit permission.
- Dismissive or adversarial attitudes toward technical contributions and reviews.
- Unprofessional conduct in team communications or academic project forums.

---

## Code Review & Discussion Etiquette

Technical disagreements are a normal part of software engineering. When debating architecture, data validation logic, or implementation patterns:
- Critique the code or technical design, never the author.
- Explain the rationale behind recommendations with practical examples or technical references.
- Seek consensus proactively, recognizing team role responsibilities (Data Pipeline, Data Quality, Orchestration, Observability, Testing, and DevOps).

---

## Scope & Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, project documentation, and team communication channels.

### Reporting Unacceptable Behavior
Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior should be reported directly to the Project Team Leader:
- **Mohammad Maher Metwaly** (Team Leader & DevOps / DQ Engineer)
- Or brought to the attention of academic program mentors and supervisors.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion, fairness, and confidentiality. Team members found violating these standards may face corrective action or escalation to university project advisors.
